This project uses three n8n workflows to identify businesses in the United Kingdom that are either opening soon or have recently opened. It generates targeted searches to locate opening announcements via Google (facilitated by SerpApi) and stores the results in the Google Sheets. Basic filters flag irrelevant or outdated entries, while human review determines which candidates proceed to the next stage. Approved businesses then undergo website discovery and the enrichment of public contact information. Duplicate checks and clear progress-tracking mechanisms keep the research organised, with the goal of turning scattered opening announcements into actionable B2B intelligence.
